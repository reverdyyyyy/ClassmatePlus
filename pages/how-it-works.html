The short version

No money changes hands. Just skills, traded directly.

ClassmatePlus exists because your AI tools can debug your code, but they can't teach you guitar chords, fix your portfolio, or rehearse your interview with you. Students can.

01

List your skills

Register and add the skills you can teach — as many or as few as you want — plus what you're hoping to learn. This becomes your profile.

02

Browse or get matched

Search Browse Skills by category or keyword, or check your Dashboard's Potential Matches — students whose "wanted" list overlaps with what you offer.

03

Send a swap request

Propose trading one of your skills for one of theirs. They can accept, and you two work out the logistics — same dining hall, video call, whatever's easiest.

04

Track it in your Dashboard

Sent and received requests, accepted swaps, and completed ones all live in one place so nothing gets lost in a group chat.

A few ground rules

  • No payment of any kind — this is a trade board, not a marketplace.
  • Swaps are between two students; ClassmatePlus just makes the introduction.
  • Be upfront about your actual skill level — "beginner Photoshop" is still worth offering.
  • If a swap falls through, just cancel the request — no penalty, no awkwardness.
